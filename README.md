### CLIM-000057: Attribution of the residual land carbon sink
- **Subfield:** Terrestrial and marine carbon cycles
- **Open:** Separate CO₂ fertilization, nutrient deposition, climate, regrowth, and management contributions to regional uptake. Predict withheld fluxes and vegetation-versus-soil storage consistently.
- **Status:** A decades-old problem; O’Sullivan et al. (2022) showed globally agreeing models diverge in processes and regional responses.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Determines future vegetation and soil uptake of emissions.
- **Key works:** O’Sullivan et al. (2022), [“Process-oriented analysis of dominant sources of uncertainty in the land carbon sink”](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-32416-8).
- **Related:** Inverse-problem identifiability; attribution under interacting treatments.
